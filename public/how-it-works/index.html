An orchestration of trust,
from start to finish.

Whether you are entrusting us with family or joining our certified roster of caregivers, explore the end-to-end timeline designed for complete clarity.

Phase 01 Before Departure

The Briefing & Intake

You share your pet's precise profile with your dedicated concierge—covering daily routines, medical history, dietary rules, and behavioral nuances.

  • Comprehensive pet dossier creation
  • Custom care plan definition
  • Concierge consultation
Phase 02 Curated Selection

Targeted Matching & Meet

Rather than browsing public catalogs, your concierge handpicks sitters from our certified roster. You review detailed profiles and confirm fit through an introductory meet-and-greet.

  • No open directory searching
  • 100% Zoélys Certified caregivers
  • In-person or virtual intro meet
Phase 03 During The Stay

Daily Transparency & Portal

While you travel, your private member portal logs every walk, meal, and medication in real time, accompanied by photo updates and direct messaging.

  • Real-time daily care logs
  • Integrated 24/7 digital veterinary backup
  • Concierge monitoring behind the scenes
Phase 04 The Return

Peace of Mind Homecoming

Return to a relaxed, happy pet and an immaculate home. Complete booking confirmation effortlessly through your credit subscription.

  • Transparent escrow credit billing
  • Final stay summary report
  • Re-book favorite sitters with 1 click
Phase 01 Application

Audit & Commitment Offset

Submit your experience and references. An upfront €30 compliance fee covers identity validation and background audits—100% reimbursed across your first completed sittings.

  • Automated identity & background screening
  • Intent-focused screening pipeline
  • Full €30 commission offset upon onboarding
Phase 02 Evaluation

NAPPS Fast-Track & Scenarios

Certified veterinary professionals skip theoretical exams directly to scenario evaluation. Uncertified candidates complete real-world modules testing reactivity and health emergencies.

  • Professional license fast-track option
  • Reactivity & separation anxiety scenarios
  • Emergency response evaluation
Phase 03 Interview

Concierge Video Assessment

A structured 15-minute live video interview with our team to evaluate empathy, communication skills, composure under stress, and quiet-luxury standards.

  • 1-on-1 human verification
  • Empathy & crisis decision review
  • Quiet-luxury service alignment
Phase 04 Certification

Certification & Roster Active

Earn the official *Zoélys Certified* badge, unlock curated client pairings, set your availability, and enjoy guaranteed escrow payouts for every completed stay.

  • Zoélys Certified roster badge
  • Curated matching (no bidding wars)
  • Guaranteed escrow payout protection
