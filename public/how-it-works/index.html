THE ZOÉLYS STANDARD

How it works

Two principles guide every decision: rigorous vetting, and a personal introduction before any pet is ever entrusted.

THE VETTING PROCESS

Five stages. One badge.

1

Application

Detailed profile, experience, references and a complete home setup review.

2

Background check

Full identity verification and criminal record check — no exceptions.

3

Zoélys interview

A personal video interview assessing values, empathy and fit with our standards.

4

Trial period

Monitored first sittings with continuous ratings review.

5

Certified badge awarded

Only those who pass every stage earn the Zoélys Certified badge.

THE MEET-AND-GREET

No booking until your pet approves

Before any booking is confirmed, the sitter meets your pet in person. Once complete, they earn a Zoélys Verified Meet badge for that pet.

"This is what makes Zoélys different. No booking is confirmed until your pet approves."

DURING THE SITTING

Four premium features

Structured pet profile

A full briefing sent to your sitter before day one — every critical detail flagged by priority.

Live updates on demand

Photos and videos at the frequency you choose, not your sitter.

Partner vet on standby

A trusted vet is one call away if anything goes wrong.

Post-sitting report

A full summary, so you come home informed — not anxious.
