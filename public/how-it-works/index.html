The Zoélys Standard

How it works

Zoélys is a private caregiving concierge for pets. You hand your concierge a care brief, your dates, and your budget. They study the dossier, handpick the caregivers who truly fit, and you approve the one you like.

One platform, two journeys

Cared for on both sides.

The owner journey
Your concierge, start to finish
1

Tell us about your pet and dates

Your care brief. A guided intake for temperament, medications, habits, and your dates. It takes about two minutes.

2

Your concierge approves and handpicks

Your concierge studies the dossier, approves the match privately, and handpicks a small set of caregivers who truly fit. Never a public roster to browse.

3

You review and confirm

Fitting caregivers appear privately in your portal. Care plans, photo galleries, and a daily care log sit beside each profile. Confirm the one you trust.

4

Your caregiver accepts, care begins

The sitting is locked and care begins. Your pet stays in familiar, trusted hands.

5

Follow the care log and the wallet

Follow the daily care log from anywhere. Your caregiver’s payout is settled instantly into a personal wallet they can withdraw anytime.

The caregiver journey
Five-stage authorization
1

Application

Detailed profile, verifiable experience, references and a home-setup review.

2

Background check

Full identity verification and criminal record check. No exceptions.

3

Zoélys interview

A personal video interview assessing values, empathy and fit with our standards.

4

Trial period

Monitored first sittings with continuous rating review.

5

Authorized caregiver

Pass every stage and earn the Zoélys Authorized Caregiver badge. Then start receiving matched requests privately in your portal. When a sitting locks, your payout settles instantly into your wallet.
