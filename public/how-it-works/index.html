The Vetting Process

Five stages. One badge.

1

Application

Detailed profile, experience, references and a complete home setup review.

2

Background check

Full identity verification and criminal record check — no exceptions.

3

Zoélys interview

A personal video interview assessing values, empathy and fit with our standards.

4

Trial period

Monitored first sittings with continuous ratings review.

5

Certified badge awarded

Only those who pass every stage earn the Zoélys Certified badge.
