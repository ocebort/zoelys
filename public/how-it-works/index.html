How it works

Cared for on both sides.

Zoélys is a private caregiving concierge for pets. You hand your concierge a care brief, your dates, and your budget. They study the dossier, handpick the caregivers who truly fit, and you approve the one you like.

The Zoélys standard

Comfort is the minimum

Comfort is the minimum. A full day of care brings at least two walks, meals exactly as written, and medication on schedule, all folded into one quiet figure. Only what your pet truly needs beyond that, a moment with the groomer or the vet, is ever extra.

What is extra at Zoélys is only what your pet truly needs beyond that baseline: grooming, and visits to the vet. Everything essential, food, medication, walks, and comfort, is part of the agreed price, never an add-on.
