Pricing

Clear, quiet pricing

One quiet membership, a steady number of credits each month, and a concierge who handles the rest. What the tier promises is what the month delivers.

Zoélys runs on a monthly rhythm. Choose a tier, receive your credits each month, and spend them on genuine care from a roster your concierge has already vetted and approved. There is nothing to compare at pickup time, no bills to chase, no arithmetic to do.

One credit is €5, always: a €50 sitter rate appears as 10 credits, a €75 rate as 15. Every sitter sets their own price inside a published range, and the figure you approve is the figure you pay.

The Zoélys standard

Comfort is the minimum. A full day of care brings at least two walks, meals exactly as written, and medication on schedule, all folded into one quiet figure. Only what your pet truly needs beyond that, a moment with the groomer or the vet, is ever extra.

In the open

What is never charged

A single, honest figure

The price you approve before a booking is the price you pay. When it settles, the caregiver is paid from that same envelope the moment they accept, and the line appears in your ledger: total, what they received, and what kept Zoélys running. Nothing moves in the dark.

What is never charged

  • Booking or service fees on top of the approved price
  • Surge pricing during holidays or busy nights
  • Fees to contact your concierge
  • Anything your approved figure did not include
Getting started

Members start with credits already in hand

First month, covered

Every new member account opens with a starter gift of credits, enough to book your first sitting and feel how the system works before your first billing month arrives.

Prefer no commitment?

Credits also come in packs: 10 for €50, 20 for €100, 50 for €250 with 5 added free, and 100 for €500 with 10 added free. Packs never expire and carry no monthly minimum, for the months your rhythm is lighter.
