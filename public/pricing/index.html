How Zoélys Pricing Operates

We believe in total transparency. The price you approve before a booking is the exact figure you pay. We never charge:

  • Booking or service fees on top of the approved price
  • Surge pricing during holidays or busy weekends
  • Fees to contact or message your concierge
  • Hidden add-ons not included in your approved briefing

For months when your travel rhythm is lighter, credits can also be purchased in flexible non-subscription packs that never expire:

  • 10 Credits: €50
  • 20 Credits: €100
  • 50 Credits: €250 (+ 5 bonus credits free)
  • 100 Credits: €500 (+ 10 bonus credits free)

Comfort is our minimum baseline. Every full day of care brings at least two comprehensive walks, scheduled feedings exactly as written, and medication administered on time. Only extra requests—like a visit to the groomer or vet—are billed separately.

Every new member account opens with a starter gift of credits, allowing you to book your first sitting and experience how the system works before your first billing month arrives.
