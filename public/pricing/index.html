Pricing

Clear, quiet pricing

You set a budget. Your concierge stays inside it. There are no surge fees, no surprise line items, and no price games at pickup time.

Care on Zoélys runs on Strategic Credits. Every sitting costs a small number of credits depending on the nightly budget you choose, and the caregiver you approve is paid in full from that same envelope, straight into their wallet the moment they accept. What you plan is what you pay.

What a sitting costs

One rate, decided by your budget

Under $50
1 credit per night
Quiet, essential care. Walks, feeding, medication on schedule, and the daily care log.
$50 to $99
2 credits per night
Our most chosen tier. Experienced caregivers with vet-tech or specialist backgrounds, plus photo updates and full care planning.
$100 to $149
2 credits per night
Senior and high-need pets. Medication management, separation-anxiety care, and a concierge who checks in personally.
$150 and above
3 credits per night
The full white-glove tier. Premium hourly enrichment, vet-tech caregivers, and priority concierge support all hours.

Credits buy the whole sitting, not a percentage. The caregiver receives the full nightly rate you agreed to; your credit balance simply deducts the published cost for your tier. Unused credits never expire while your membership is active.

Where your credit goes

One envelope, three honest hands

To the caregiver

The largest share. Your caregiver earns their full nightly rate into a personal wallet the moment they accept, and can withdraw it anytime, like any payout they own.

To the vetting

A share funds what makes Zoélys different: in-person interviews, background checks, certification verification, insurance checks, and the oversight that keeps the roster small.

To the concierge

A share runs your private concierge desk. The human who studies your pet's dossier, negotiates nothing you did not approve, and stays reachable while care is happening.

What is never charged

  • Booking or service fees on top of your rate
  • Surge pricing during holidays
  • Fees to contact your concierge
  • Anything your budget did not approve
Getting started

Members start with credits already in hand

First sitting, covered

Every new member account opens with a starter gift of credits, enough to book an early sitting and feel how the system works before you ever add more.

Top-ups on your terms

When you run low, your concierge adds the credits you ask for, no subscription lock-in and no monthly minimum. For large or recurring needs, members keep a standing balance the concierge monitors.
