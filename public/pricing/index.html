How Zoélys Pricing Operates

We believe in total transparency. The price you approve before a booking is the exact figure you pay. We never charge:

  • Booking or service fees on top of the approved price
  • Surge pricing during holidays or busy weekends
  • Fees to contact or message your concierge
  • Hidden add-ons not included in your approved briefing

For Non-Subscribers: A la carte credit packs are available at €5.50/credit (€55 for 10, €110 for 20), or bulk packs at €4.55/credit (€250 for 55, €500 for 110).

For Active Subscribers: Small top-ups (< 20 credits) are €5.50/credit. Large top-ups (≥ 20 credits) automatically unlock your subscription's discounted rate (€4.95 for Gold, €4.79 for Platinum, and €4.54 for Diamond).

Comfort is our minimum baseline across all tiers. Every full day of care brings at least two comprehensive walks, scheduled feedings exactly as written, 24/7 veterinary backup, and medication administered on time.

Every new member account opens with a starter gift of credits, allowing you to book your first sitting and experience how the system works before your first billing month arrives.
