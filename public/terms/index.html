Platform Overview

1. Waiting List & Pre-Launch Status

Submitting an intake form or joining the Zoélys private waitlist does not constitute a binding service contract, an escrow transaction, or a guarantee of immediate sitter placement. Service availability depends on concierge matching and regional launch timelines in Miami, Florida.

2. Independent Contractor Relationship (Fla. Stat. § 443.1216)

Caregivers on the Zoélys roster operate as independent business entities. They maintain operational independence over their sitting practices while adhering strictly to Zoélys verification, safety standards, and client care plan guidelines.

3. Member Eligibility & Accurate Dossiers

Members agree to provide accurate information regarding their pet's health, temperament, vaccination records, and medical conditions during concierge intake. Zoélys reserves the right to decline service if a pet poses an unmanaged safety risk to caregivers or other animals.

4. Emergency Medical Authorizations

Prior to booking a sitting service, pet owners sign an explicit Emergency Medical Care Authorization Form granting authorized caregivers limited proxy to seek urgent veterinary care when the owner is unreachable, subject to designated spending limits.

5. Home Access & Audio Privacy Compliance

Members providing access to private residences must disclose key codes, access protocols, and the presence of security devices. In compliance with Florida two-party consent laws (Fla. Stat. § 934.03), audio-recording devices in private sitting quarters must be explicitly disclosed prior to any stay.
