Premium is the people we let through.

To ensure absolute safety, Zoélys uses a structured four-stage vetting protocol. By combining automated compliance checks, professional certification fast-tracking, and live human review, we protect your peace of mind without compromise.

01

Identity & Safety Verification

Automated identity checks and criminal background screening through secure databases. An upfront commitment fee screens for dedicated caregivers and is reimbursed upon first bookings.

02

Professional Fast-Track & Testing

Grounded in NAPPS standards. Caregivers holding active veterinary technician or professional pet care licenses bypass theoretical testing directly into scenario evaluation.

03

Real-World Scenario Assessment

Virtual situational testing evaluating decision-making during real-life events, including dog reactivity on walks, separation anxiety, and emergency responses.

04

Live Concierge Video Interview

A 15-minute structured video evaluation assessing professionalism, empathy, and calm crisis management before granting full Zoélys Certification.
