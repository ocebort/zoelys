Trust & Safety

The Zoélys Standard

Trust is not claimed here. It is built, credential by credential, layer by layer. Zoélys is a private caregiving concierge for pets with a small, vetted roster of certified professionals.

The Pipeline

From Application to Authorized Caregiver

01

Application & Screening

Every candidacy begins with a written application and a one-on-one interview. We review experience, neighborhood familiarity, species comfort, and special-care capabilities. Then we run full identity and criminal background screening before anything moves forward.

02

Certification Review

Recognized credentials are verified directly at the issuing source—including CPPS (Certified Professional Pet Sitter), Pet First Aid & CPR, and Veterinary Technician licensure. Certification earns respect, never automatic entry.

03

Practical Scenario Assessment

A hands-on virtual and situational evaluation with real scenario testing. We assess what résumés cannot: calm presence under pressure, reading an anxious animal's subtle signals, medication handling, and quiet composure.

04

Insurance Verification

Before approval, every caregiver must show proof of active, professional pet-sitting liability coverage. Policies are verified for validity and currency, then re-checked periodically throughout their time on the roster.

05

Zoélys Authorized Status

Authorized caregivers receive the Zoélys badge, appear on our private concierge roster, and maintain active care plans and daily care logs. They remain under ongoing oversight, with periodic re-verification of credentials and care quality.

Two Layers of Protection

Coverage & Platform Accountability

Layer One

Caregiver Professional Policy

Each authorized caregiver operates as an independent, certified professional carrying their own commercial pet-sitting liability insurance. It is a mandatory condition of roster entry, protecting their care practice, the animal, and your home.

Layer Two

Platform Commercial Backstop

Zoélys maintains commercial general liability coverage as the concierge platform. We never hide behind technicalities—accountability travels with the brand that curated the match, supported by our concierge desk from report to resolution.

If Something Happens

A Rehearsed Emergency Protocol

Protocol 01

You are Called First

Your contact information and preferred emergency veterinarian details are verified before day one. If anything strays from the care plan, immediate direct contact is initiated without delay.

Protocol 02

The Approved Plan Governs

Caregivers execute precisely what you wrote down: authorized veterinarians, medication dosages, and quiet care routines. Zero improvisation where a written care plan exists.

Protocol 03

Incident Report Within the Hour

What happened is documented immediately while details are fresh, accompanied by photo documentation where permitted, and reviewed directly with you before next steps are taken.

Protocol 04

Concierge Administrative Support

Our concierge desk guides the documentation process through applicable insurance channels and stays beside you until the situation is completely resolved.

In Your Home

On Your Terms, On Your Schedule

Keys and smart lock codes are logged and returned immediately upon completion of a sitting. No copies are ever retained, and every entry and exit time is automatically logged within your daily care portal.

If you maintain security or nanny cameras in your home, simply note their presence in your written Care Plan. In compliance with Florida privacy laws (Fla. Stat. § 934.03), audio-recording devices in private areas must be disclosed prior to the start of a sitting.

Credentials, criminal background checks, and liability policies are re-verified on a continuous schedule. Authorized status is maintained through active evidence and revoked immediately if standards are ever unfulfilled.
