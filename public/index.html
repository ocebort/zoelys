The Zoélys Standard

Premium is the people we let through.

Every caregiver is meticulously evaluated. Sitters holding recognized professional veterinary or advanced animal care certifications may bypass preliminary screening stages to enter directly into the trial evaluation.

01

Application & Dossier Review

Detailed professional history, verified references, and home-setup review. Certified veterinary professionals fast-track directly past this initial stage.

02

Comprehensive Background Check

Full identity verification, criminal record screening, and safety audit on every approved caregiver candidate without exception.

03

Zoélys Concierge Interview

Rigorous personal video evaluation testing situational judgment, empathy, crisis response, and alignment with quiet-luxury hospitality standards.

04

Trial Period & Certified Badge

Monitored live sittings and client feedback reviews before earning the official Zoélys Certified badge and entering the active roster.
