The Zoélys Standard

Premium is the people we let through.

Every caregiver is interviewed, background checked, and vetted in person. Every booking is held to the same standard. No exceptions.

01

Application

A detailed profile, verifiable experience, references and a complete home-setup review.

02

Background check

Full identity verification and criminal record check on every sitter. No exceptions.
