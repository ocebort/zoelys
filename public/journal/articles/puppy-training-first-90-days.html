How to train your puppy — the first 90 days

By Océane Bort • 12 min read

The first three months of a puppy’s life in your home shape their nervous system, habit loops, and confidence for the next fifteen years. Rather than overwhelming a young mind with complex tricks, focus on three pillars: routine predictability, safe socialization, and calm bite inhibition.

Consistency in timing—waking, feeding, potty breaks, and quiet rest—builds security far faster than verbal commands alone.
