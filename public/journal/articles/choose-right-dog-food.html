How to choose the right food for your dog

BY OCÉANE BORT • 10 MIN READ

Pet food marketing is flooded with buzzwords like 'holistic', 'ancestral', and 'human-grade'. To navigate the aisles effectively, learn to inspect the guaranteed analysis panel and ingredient sourcing rather than front-of-bag claims.

Every dog has unique metabolic requirements based on age, coat condition, energy levels, and digestive sensitivities.
