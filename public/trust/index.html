Trust & Safety

The Zoélys Standard

Trust is not claimed here. It is built, credential by credential, layer by layer.

Zoélys is a private caregiving concierge for pets. The roster stays deliberately small and exceptional, so every caregiver is interviewed, background checked, and vetted in person before ever meeting an animal. We verify professional certifications, observe real handling, confirm active liability coverage, and only then grant the status that matters: Zoélys Authorized Caregiver.

The Pipeline

From application to authorized caregiver

01

Application & Screening

Every candidacy begins with a written application and a one-on-one interview. We review experience, neighbourhood, species comfort, and special-care capabilities. Then we run identity and background screening before anything else moves forward.

02

Certification Review

Recognized credentials are verified at the source. CPPS (Certified Professional Pet Sitter), pet first aid & CPR, veterinary technician licensure. A certification earns respect, never automatic entry. It must survive the stages that follow.

03

Practical Assessment

Hands-on evaluation with real animals. We assess what résumés cannot: calm presence under pressure, reading an anxious animal's signals, medication handling, and the quiet attentions that define our philosophy of care.

04

Insurance Verification

Before approval, every sitter must show proof of active professional pet-sitting liability insurance. Policies are checked for validity and currency, then re-verified periodically throughout their time on the roster.

05

Zoélys Authorized Caregiver

The final status. Authorized caregivers receive the Zoélys badge, appear on the private roster and map, and their profiles carry care plans, photo galleries, and a daily care log. They remain under ongoing oversight, with periodic re-verification of credentials, coverage, and care quality.

Authorized Status
Coverage

Two layers of protection

Layer One. The Caregiver

Sitter's own policy

Each authorized caregiver carries their own professional pet-sitting liability insurance, protecting their business if a pet, owner, or property is harmed while in their care. It is a condition of entry, not an option.

Layer Two. The Platform

Zoélys' own coverage

Zoélys maintains its own commercial liability coverage as the platform. We never hide behind "the sitter is responsible". Accountability travels with the brand that made the match.

The Fine Print, Plainly

What is covered, and what is not

Injury to a pet in our care
Liability responds where the caregiver’s own policy carries the incident. What matters to us more than the clause is the response: a phone call to you within the hour, never a claim number alone.
Mishaps in your home
Accidents to your property are handled the same way. Our concierge stays with the incident from first report until it settles, so you are never translating insurance paperwork on your own.
Prevention comes first
Protection is a backstop, not the product. The real safeguards happen before anything happens: written care plans, daily logs, vet permissions on file, and a caregiver who has already met your animal.
What is not covered
Pre-existing conditions, care chosen outside your written care plan, and incidents reported late. Plain language matters to us, so the full terms are sent to you before a booking, never after one.
If Something Happens

A protocol we rehearse, not improvise

Protocol 01

You are called first

Your number and your vet’s number are on file before day one. When something goes off plan, one of those two calls happens at once, with you first.

Protocol 02

The approved plan governs

The caregiver acts on what you wrote down: the vet, the medication, the quiet protocol. No improvisation where a written plan already exists.

Protocol 03

An incident report within the hour

What happened is written down while it is fresh, photographs where you allow them, and the next step is agreed with you before anything else happens.

Protocol 04

We settle it through

Our team routes the incident through the caregiver’s policy or our own platform coverage, and stays with you until resolution, not until the paperwork lands.

In Your Home

On your terms, on your schedule

Your keys stay yours

Keys are returned and logged the moment a sitting ends. No copies held, no quiet retention, and every entry into your home appears in the daily log your caregiver keeps for you.

Nothing behind closed doors

Care follows the care plan: feeding, walks, medication, calm time. The caregiver moves through your home the way you would, and the log always tells the truth about it.

Cameras, if you run them

If you keep cameras at home, say so in the care plan. The caregiver plans around them kindly, and we record the arrangement so there is no surprise on either side.

Oversight never stops

Credentials and liability policies are re-verified periodically, not assumed. Authorized status is renewed by evidence, and revoked quietly when it stops arriving.
