Trust & Safety

The Zoélys Standard

Trust is not claimed here. It is built, credential by credential, layer by layer.

Zoélys is a private caregiving concierge for pets. The roster stays deliberately small and exceptional, so every caregiver is interviewed, background checked, and vetted in person before ever meeting an animal. We verify professional certifications, observe real handling, confirm active liability coverage, and only then grant the status that matters: Zoélys Authorized Caregiver.

The Pipeline

From application to authorized caregiver

01

Application & Screening

Every candidacy begins with a written application and a one-on-one interview. We review experience, neighbourhood, species comfort, and special-care capabilities. Then we run identity and background screening before anything else moves forward.

02

Certification Review

Recognized credentials are verified at the source. CPPS (Certified Professional Pet Sitter), pet first aid & CPR, veterinary technician licensure. A certification earns respect, never automatic entry. It must survive the stages that follow.

03

Practical Assessment

Hands-on evaluation with real animals. We assess what résumés cannot: calm presence under pressure, reading an anxious animal's signals, medication handling, and the quiet attentions that define our philosophy of care.

04

Insurance Verification

Before approval, every sitter must show proof of active professional pet-sitting liability insurance. Policies are checked for validity and currency, then re-verified periodically throughout their time on the roster.

05

Zoélys Authorized Caregiver

The final status. Authorized caregivers receive the Zoélys badge, appear on the private roster and map, and their profiles carry care plans, photo galleries, and a daily care log. They remain under ongoing oversight, with periodic re-verification of credentials, coverage, and care quality.

Authorized Status
Coverage

Two layers of protection

Layer One. The Caregiver

Sitter's own policy

Each authorized caregiver carries their own professional pet-sitting liability insurance, protecting their business if a pet, owner, or property is harmed while in their care. It is a condition of entry, not an option.

Layer Two. The Platform

Zoélys' own coverage

Zoélys maintains its own commercial liability coverage as the platform. We never hide behind "the sitter is responsible". Accountability travels with the brand that made the match.
