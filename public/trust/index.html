Emergency Protocol

A Rehearsed Emergency Escalation Chain

Step 01

Immediate Owner Contact

If any health or safety concern arises during a sitting, the caregiver initiates direct contact with you immediately via phone call and in-app emergency alert.

Step 02

Integrated 24/7 Tele-Vet Triage

If you are unreachable or unable to answer, your signed Member Authorization permits instant consultation via our integrated 24/7 Veterinary API—included in your membership at zero extra charge to evaluate urgency with a licensed vet.

Step 03

Designated Vet Transport

If triage confirms medical urgency, your pet is transported immediately to your designated preferred veterinary clinic (or the nearest 24/7 emergency hospital if your primary clinic is closed).

Step 04

Formal Incident Documentation

A full, timestamped Incident Report—including photo documentation, vet triage logs, and next steps—is compiled and posted directly to your portal for complete transparency.

Insurance & Accountability

Mandatory Protection Standards

Mandatory

Caregiver Insurance

Every Zoélys Authorized Caregiver must hold active Professional Pet Sitting & Commercial Liability Insurance (minimum $1,000,000 coverage). Proof is verified prior to authorization and audited periodically.

Member Partner

Pet Owner Protection

While our baseline care includes 24/7 vet triage, members are encouraged to maintain active Pet Health Insurance. For owners without coverage, we partner directly with licensed Florida pet insurance providers at setup.

Platform Backstop

Platform Liability

Zoélys maintains commercial general liability coverage as the platform concierge. Our team personally coordinates documentation and stays with you from initial report through claim settlement.

Legal & Home Privacy

Clear Terms, Plain Language

Legally, veterinary clinics cannot perform non-routine medical care requested by a non-owner without written consent. Before your first stay, every Zoélys member signs an electronic Emergency Medical Care Authorization Form.

This agreement grants your authorized caregiver and our concierge desk limited medical proxy to seek emergency vet care if you are unreachable, governed by a pre-approved emergency spending limit and designated clinic permissions.

If you operate security or nanny cameras, simply note their presence in your written Care Plan. In compliance with Florida two-party consent privacy laws (Fla. Stat. § 934.03), audio-recording devices in private sitting quarters must be explicitly disclosed prior to a sitting.

Caregivers on Zoélys operate as independent, certified micro-entrepreneurs. They maintain operational independence over their sitting practices while adhering strictly to Zoélys safety standards, verification audits, and client briefing instructions.

Keys and smart lock codes are logged and returned immediately upon completion of a sitting. No copies are ever retained, and entry/exit timestamps are automatically recorded within your daily care portal.
