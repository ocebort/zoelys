Legal & Data Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Our Core Promise

Zoélys respects your home and family. We never sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or pet data to third-party advertisers or brokers. Ever.

1. Information We Collect

When you join the Zoélys waitlist or submit a concierge intake request ("Find a Sitter"), we collect only the personal information strictly necessary to prepare your profile and deliver concierge matching services in Miami, Florida:

  • Contact Information: Your name and email address.
  • Pet Dossier Data: Pet species, breed, age, medical conditions, diet routines, and stranger comfort levels.
  • Location Context: Your Miami neighborhood or area (e.g., Brickell, Coconut Grove) to assign local caregivers.
  • Technical Logs: Standard web request information such as IP address and browser type collected automatically by our hosting infrastructure to protect against spam and abuse.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for operational and client relationship purposes:

  • To process your waitlist position and send early-access welcome briefings.
  • To analyze neighborhood demand and match your household with certified caregivers.
  • To send administrative emails, safety updates, or concierge follow-up messages regarding your requested stay dates.

3. Technical Infrastructure & Third-Party Processors

To deliver a secure, fast digital experience, Zoélys relies on trusted, industry-leading infrastructure partners. Your information is processed through:

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare D1 & Workers): Secure serverless database hosting and web routing. Cloudflare encrypts stored data and processes traffic to defend against cyber threats.
  • Transactional Email Services (Resend / SendGrid): Email delivery platforms used exclusively to send your waitlist confirmations, onboarding briefs, and concierge messages.

These third-party processors handle your data strictly under our instruction and are legally bound by rigorous confidentiality and data protection standards.

4. Email Rights & Unsubscribing

Every email sent by Zoélys includes a direct, 1-click Unsubscribe link in the footer. You may opt out of marketing or waitlist updates at any time. (Essential operational or safety alerts related to active sittings will still be delivered if you are an active booking client).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your waitlist information only as long as necessary to fulfill your concierge request. If you wish to have your email or pet dossier permanently removed from our Cloudflare D1 database prior to our launch, simply email your request to concierge@zoelys.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or our data protection practices in Miami, Florida, please contact our concierge team at concierge@zoelys.com.
